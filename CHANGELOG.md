# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Builds a one-page A4 circulation slip from the details you enter, with a live preview.
- Registered, stamp/lodging and e-Filing reference routes.
- Searchable snapshot of the official case-type list, kept separate from the circulation category.
- Optional FRESH MATTER marking.
- Blocks printing when the synopsis or the slip would not fit on one page.
- macOS desktop application for Apple silicon, with its own icon, About panel and menu.
- Privacy policy inside the application and in `PRIVACY.md`.
- Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
